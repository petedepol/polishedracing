# Media Research Report — polishedracing.co.uk
**Pete Michaliszyn ("Polish Pete") — Photo & Video Asset Guide**
*Research compiled: February 2026*

---

## Overview

Pete Michaliszyn — known throughout the mountain bike world as **"Polish Pete"** — has a career spanning over 20 years, from Polish junior road racing through the UK DH scene, into World Cup mechanics with the Atherton Racing family, and on to Olympic-level work with INEOS Grenadiers and Cannondale Factory Racing. This report identifies every known media source where Pete appears, plus archives, photographers, and social media accounts to contact for licensed images.

---

## Career Phases — Media by Era

### Phase 1 — UK DH Racing Scene (approx. 2005–2009)
Pete raced himself, with results documented on **Roots & Rain** (rootsandrain.co.uk) as far back as 2005. He appears in a **2009 BikeRadar article** alongside the Mojo Suspension team that included Dan Stanbridge, Ben Cathro, and Chris Hutchens. This is the earliest documented media era.

**Sources to check:**
- Roots & Rain (rootsandrain.co.uk) — search "Michaliszyn" for early race results and any associated photos
- BikeRadar 2009 — Mojo Suspension team feature
- Dirt Mountain Bike magazine archives (2006–2009) — Mojo Suspension team coverage

### Phase 2 — Atherton Racing / GT Factory Racing & Trek Factory Racing DH (approx. 2011–2019)
This is by far the richest era for media. Pete was Gee Atherton's personal mechanic for approximately **7 years** (confirmed in Mountain Bike Action, 2017 — "Pete Michaliszyn wrenches for Gee for his 7th year"). He was also featured prominently in **The Atherton Project** video series on Red Bull TV, where fans nicknamed him a fan favourite.

**The Atherton Project (Red Bull TV) — CRITICAL SOURCE:**

Pete features in multiple episodes of **The Atherton Project**, Red Bull's behind-the-scenes docuseries following the Atherton family. He was popular enough with fans that they commented specifically about him — including praising him for attempting a backflip in a foam pit (Season 3, Episode 7, 2011).

- Season 1 (2009), Season 2 (2010), Season 3 (2011) — all likely contain footage and stills of Pete in the pits
- **Season 3, Episode 7** is specifically confirmed as featuring Pete (fan comment: "I am also proud of Polish Pete – he's got a great job, he is a good mechanic and as I saw in the last part of this clip he's got some skills – backflip in 7 attempts!")
- Films shot by director **Clay Porter**, photos by **Ian Hylands**
- Full series accessible at: [redbull.com — The Atherton Project](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/shows/the-atherton-project)

**Eyesdown Films bike check — HIGH PRIORITY:**

Eyesdown Films produced a dedicated **audio/video bike check with Pete** ("Polish Pete") at Val di Sole World Cup, hosted on NSMB.com. This is likely the most visual, dedicated media featuring Pete from this era. Contact Eyesdown Films directly for still frames or licensing.

## Photo Archives to Search

- "Atherton Racing" + filter by mechanic/team/pits

## Notes on Licensing

Most images found in professional cycling media are subject to copyright. For the polishedracing.co.uk website:

- **Red Bull Content Pool**: Many images are available free for editorial use with credit; commercial use requires a separate agreement
- **Getty Images / SWpix**: Standard licensing fees apply — typically per-image, per-use. Prices vary.
- **Team/sponsor photographers** (e.g. Michele Mondini): Direct negotiation — often willing to provide images to subjects featured in them, sometimes free for portfolio use
- **Screen grabs from Red Bull TV / YouTube**: Technically require permission for website use — contact Red Bull Media for clearance
- **Pete's own photos**: 100% free to use — and likely the most authentic and compelling content for the site
